Abstract

Image-conditioned video generation makes it easy to animate a single reference image, but also creates risks of unauthorized use. Existing image-immunization methods often focus on degrading visual quality or temporal coherence while recognizable source content can remain in the generated video.

I2V-DETACH reframes protection as source grounding detachment. It weakens source-to-video cue propagation while strengthening interactions among video latents, shifting denoising away from source-dominated retrieval. Across diverse diffusion architectures, the method reduces source-content preservation and exhibits strong black-box transferability.

03

Source grounding detachment

Three components redirect denoising away from the protected source image.

01

Source-divergent latents

Construct source-oriented video latents that remain informative while moving away from a source-preserving trajectory.

02

Source-video decoupling

Suppress pre-softmax attention logits from video queries to source-conditioning keys.

03

Intra-video coupling

Strengthen video-side interactions so denoising relies less on repeated retrieval from the source image.
